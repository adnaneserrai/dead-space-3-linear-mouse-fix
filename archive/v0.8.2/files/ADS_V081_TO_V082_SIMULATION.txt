DS3 Linear Mouse Fix v0.8.2 - offline ADS replay on v0.8.1 capture
=================================================================

The old v0.8.1 ADS capture did not record ADS AB9830 pre-values because the
v0.8.1 path gate recognized only the HIP parent return. Therefore this replay
validates the ADS D820 side exactly, but cannot claim an exact historical count
of ADS AB9830 bypass events from that old file.

ADS D820 caller replay: deadspace3.exe+0x00149F2E

slow R   DI sumX=+1828  actual/count=0.068220019  unclamped/count=0.068220020  clamp_samples= 0  max|preclamp_x|=0.384652
flick L  DI sumX=-2258  actual/count=0.027332172  unclamped/count=0.068085860  clamp_samples=20  max|preclamp_x|=8.717276
flick R  DI sumX=+2979  actual/count=0.023288549  unclamped/count=0.068385189  clamp_samples=23  max|preclamp_x|=11.449167
slow L   DI sumX=-2959  actual/count=0.068542222  unclamped/count=0.068542222  clamp_samples= 0  max|preclamp_x|=0.467233

Result:
- slow movements are unchanged because there are no D820 clamp samples;
- flick L has 20 D820 clamp samples and replays to ~0.068086/count without the clamp;
- flick R has 23 D820 clamp samples and replays to ~0.068385/count without the clamp;
- slow L remains ~0.068542/count.

This reproduces the independently reported ADS counterfactual closely and
supports extending the already-validated post-vanilla D820 unclamp to the ADS
caller, provided the runtime mouse-tail latch is started only by the new ADS
AB9830 fresh-mouse bypass gate.
