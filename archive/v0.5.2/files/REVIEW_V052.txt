DS3 Raw Mouse Probe v0.5.2 - initialization fix
=================================================

Root cause of v0.5.1 no-.bin failure:
- v0.5.1 resolved KERNEL32 exports named Module32FirstA and Module32NextA.
- Tool Help exposes the ANSI entry points as Module32First and Module32Next; the explicit suffixed variants are Module32FirstW / Module32NextW.
- resolve_apis() treated the two unresolved pointers as mandatory, therefore initialization stopped before DS3_RawMouseProbe.bin was created.

v0.5.2 changes:
- resolves KERNEL32!Module32First and KERNEL32!Module32Next;
- reports each missing required API to DS3_RawMouseProbe.status.txt as MISSING_API: ... before refusing;
- keeps the v5.1 binary record format unchanged;
- no Raw Input registration, no WndProc, no camera/mouse replacement;
- RegisterRawInputDevices/GetRawInputData/GetRawInputBuffer observation and D820 diagnostic behavior otherwise unchanged.

Build verification:
- clang-cl x86 /W4 /WX /Brepro
- two clean builds byte-for-byte identical
- PE32 Intel i386
